Nine views of everything you've recorded

Built for runners, walkers, tennis players and snowboarders, with cycling and sailing counted alongside.

Lifetime

An odometer for your whole history, totals per sport, and every year drawn over the others, with the years you pick.

Heatmap & explorer tiles

Every GPS track on a street map, and the map squares you've covered: how many, your largest fully explored square, new ones each year.

Repeated routes

The loops you run again and again, found on their own, with your pace on each over the years. Give them your own names.

Best efforts

Your fastest 1 km, 5 km, 10 km and half marathon inside any run, from the per-second data, and how each record moved.

Tennis court coverage

Where you move on court, from the watch's GPS: every session folded into one half court, net, mid court and baseline, forehand and backhand side.

Snowboard seasons

Days on snow, runs, vertical metres and top speed per season, and each day of each trip.

Sleep & recovery

What goes with your better and worse nights, with honest error bars, and how many nights HRV takes to settle after a hard session.

Forecasts

Tonight's sleep score and HRV, with how accurate the guess really is, and a planner for fitness and form on race day.

Year in review

One year on one page: records, places, the biggest day and week, and an image to share without giving away where you live.

How it works

01

Import your history once

Ask Garmin for your account export and drop the zip in a folder. Every original FIT file and every night of sleep, HRV and resting heart rate comes in.

02

Stay in sync every day

With your own Garmin Personal API key, new activities and last night's sleep arrive each morning, or when you press Sync now.

03

Open it in your browser

Everything is analyzed on your computer and shown at localhost, in light or dark, on a laptop or a phone.

Five commands to start

You need Docker Desktop, your Garmin export and about an hour. The setup guide walks through each step on Windows, macOS and Linux.

git clone https://github.com/mirceapert/Garmin-Lifetime-Analyzer.git
docker compose build
docker compose run --rm app gla import
docker compose up -d
docker compose run --rm app gla sync

Your data stays with you

There is no cloud service and no account to make. The app runs on your own computer and only you can open it.

  • Activities, health data and your API key are stored in one folder on your machine.
  • The only calls out are to Garmin, for your own data, and to the map tile server.
  • Want it on your phone too? The hosting checklist starts with a private network, so it never goes on the open internet.
